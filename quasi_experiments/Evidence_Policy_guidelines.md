# Evidence & Policy (Bristol University Press / Policy Press) — author guidance

Sources: journal overview page (bristoluniversitypressdigital.com/view/journals/evp/evp-overview.xml, retrieved 2026-10-30) and BUP "Instructions for Authors" (custom Harvard reference guide). Verified against scispace template summary for section structure.

## Article types
- Research articles: 5,000-7,000 words
- Review articles: 5,000-7,000 words
- Methods articles: 5,000-7,000 words
- Debate articles: 3,000-5,000 words
- Practice articles: up to 3,000 words

## Selected type: Research article
Original primary research with relevance to the relationship between evidence and policy or practice. Word maximum 7,000 INCLUDING figures and tables; guide value ~300 words per half-page figure or table. Shorter papers (~5,000) encouraged.

## Required sections (research articles)
1. Structured Abstract, up to 250 words, five labeled parts:
   - Introduction (topic, gaps, aims/research questions)
   - Methods/Data (design, sample, sources, procedures)
   - Analytic plan (analytic approach)
   - Results (primary findings)
   - Conclusion/Implications (contribution; implications for policy/practice/research)
2. Background (nature/importance of question + concise literature review + relevant theoretical/epistemological grounding)
3. Methods
4. Findings
5. Discussion
6. Conclusion

## References
Custom Harvard system:
- In-text: surname followed by year in brackets, e.g. (Smith, 2020); "and" not "&" in text citations
- Reference list entries: `Surname, A, year, Title, *Journal Name* vol, issue, pages` (titles italicized)
- Website details at end of reference; no access dates
- Spell out acronyms on first use

## Other
- British English spelling and punctuation
- Anonymous peer review; submit anonymised manuscript without author details in the main file
- Up to four key words (per BUP instructions-for-authors convention)
- Supplementary material may be uploaded separately for online-only hosting (does not count toward the word limit)
- Pre-submission inquiries welcomed by editors
