# Journal of Comparative Policy Analysis: Research and Practice (JCPA) — submission checklist

Official journal pages:

- <https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/fcpa20>
- <https://www.tandfonline.com/action/authorSubmission?show=instructions&journalCode=fcpa20>

Checked 2026-10-20. The live publisher page and Taylor & Francis submission
system remain authoritative.

## Journal fit

- JCPA publishes explicitly comparative public-policy research: comparative
  theory development and testing, comparative empirical policy research,
  comparative methods, lesson-drawing and extrapolation, and
  interdisciplinary policy analysis.
- Submissions must relate clearly to public policy and be explicitly
  comparative.
- The journal uses double-anonymous peer review.
- Open Select / hybrid: open-access publication is optional; do not assume an
  APC is required.

## Article type and length

- Original research article.
- Hard ceiling of 8,000 words unless the current Instructions for Authors
  specify a lower applicable limit; keep the full manuscript at or below
  8,000 words.
- Abstract: one paragraph (kept under 250 words).
- Keywords: 5-6.
- No more than three displayed heading levels.

## Review and manuscript formatting

- Double-anonymized main manuscript; separate title page with author
  identification.
- APA-style author-date citations; consistent alphabetical reference list.
- Figures and tables cited consecutively; self-contained captions.
- Editable figure/table files plus publication-quality images (PNG/TIFF,
  300 dpi or better).

## Required statements

- Funding, competing interests, ethics, data availability, and AI-assistance
  disclosure, kept synchronized across manuscript, title page, and
  submission-statements documents.

## This manuscript's compliance

- Explicitly comparative: structured focused comparison of Fukushima
  evacuation and Kansas tax reform organized around common dimensions;
  dedicated comparative findings section and comparative table.
- Manuscript word count is enforced at <= 8,000 by check_manuscript.py
  (JCPA config).

## Pre-upload actions

1. Recheck the live Instructions for Authors in the submission portal.
2. Confirm anonymized manuscript, separate title page, and that file
   metadata/filenames do not expose the author to reviewers.
3. Confirm declarations entered in the portal match uploaded files.
4. Preserve the final submitted archive and submission confirmation.
