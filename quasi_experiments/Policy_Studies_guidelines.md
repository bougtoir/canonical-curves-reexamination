The instructions-for-authors page was Cloudflare-gated during automated access; requirements below reflect the journal's stated peer-review model, Taylor & Francis standard author instructions, and the journal's published article format.

## Journal fit

- Policy Studies publishes interdisciplinary, comparative, historical, and global research on public policy and governance, connecting cutting-edge research to real-world policy problems.
- Original research articles predominate; the journal is traditionally political-science based but explicitly welcomes multi-disciplinary approaches.
- The journal uses **double-anonymized peer review** (initial editor screening plus two anonymous referees).
- Open access is optional through Open Select; a subscription-route submission requires no APC. Do not assume APC funding is needed.

## Article type and length

- Submit as an original research article.
- Typical articles run roughly 6,000-9,000 words all-inclusive; keep the manuscript within approximately 9,000 words.
- Abstract: one paragraph, approximately 150-200 words.
- Keywords: 5-6.
- No more than three displayed heading levels; automatic page numbers; Times New Roman or a comparable plain font.

## Review and manuscript formatting

- Prepare a double-anonymized main manuscript and a separate title page carrying author identification.
- Author-date (APA-style) citations and a consistent alphabetical reference list.
- Cite figures and tables consecutively in the text; provide self-contained captions.
- Submit editable figure and table files in addition to publication-quality figure images; TIFF/PNG at 300 dpi or better.
- Use Word-native (OMML) equations rather than images for mathematical notation.

## Required statements

- Include a data availability statement for original research.
- Provide funding, competing-interests, and author-contribution statements.
- Taylor & Francis policy requires disclosure of AI assistance beyond copy editing; the human author remains responsible for verification, interpretation, and the submitted text. Keep the synchronized AI-assistance declaration consistent across manuscript, title page, and submission statements.
- Enter declarations in the submission interface where requested; uploaded statements do not replace required metadata.

## Files prepared by this project

- Anonymized inline manuscript and a separate-figure upload variant.
- Separate appendix.
- Separate title page, cover letter, and submission-statements document.
- Editable tables DOCX and figure/table PPTX.
- Numbered PNG and TIFF figures.
- Reproduction README, generated numerical manifest, validation report, and source files.

## Pre-upload actions

1. Recheck the live word, abstract, keyword, figure, table, and declaration instructions in the submission portal.
2. Upload or link an anonymized data/code archive for reviewers.
3. Confirm that repository metadata, document properties, filenames, and the title page are not exposed to anonymous reviewers.
4. Confirm that all declarations entered in the submission system match the uploaded files.
5. Preserve the final submitted archive and submission-system confirmation.
