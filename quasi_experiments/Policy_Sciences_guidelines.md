# Policy Sciences submission-guideline checklist

Official journal instructions:

- <https://link.springer.com/journal/11077/submission-guidelines>
- <https://link.springer.com/journal/11077>

Checked on 2026-10-01. The live publisher page and the Springer Nature submission system remain authoritative.

## Article type and length

- Submit as an **Analysis** article: the manuscript develops a policy-science concept and analytical sequence, using empirical cases as demonstrations.
- Research and analysis articles are generally 8,500-9,500 words.
- The strict maximum is 10,000 words, including the main text, footnotes, acknowledgements, and references.
- Abstract: 150-250 words.
- Keywords: 4-6.

## Review and manuscript formatting

- Prepare a double-anonymous main manuscript and a separate title page.
- Use a plain font, automatic page numbering, and no more than three displayed heading levels.
- Use Word equation objects rather than images for equations.
- Use author-date citations and a consistent reference list.
- Cite figures and tables consecutively in the text and provide self-contained captions.
- Supply editable source files in addition to publication-quality figure files.

## Required statements

- Include a Data Availability statement for original research.
- Provide funding, competing-interests, author-contribution, and applicable ethics statements.
- Large language models are not authors. Assistance beyond copy editing must be disclosed, and the human author remains responsible for source verification, analysis, interpretation, and the submitted text.
- Enter declarations in the submission interface where requested; uploaded statements do not replace required metadata.

## Files prepared by this project

- Anonymized inline manuscript and a separate-figure upload variant.
- Separate appendix.
- Separate title page, cover letter, and submission-statements document.
- Editable tables DOCX and figure/table PPTX.
- Numbered PNG and TIFF figures.
- Reproduction README, generated numerical manifest, validation report, and source files.

## Pre-upload actions

1. Recheck the live word, abstract, keyword, figure, table, and declaration instructions.
2. Upload or link an anonymized data/code archive for reviewers.
3. Confirm that repository metadata, document properties, filenames, and the title page are not exposed to anonymous reviewers.
4. Confirm that all declarations entered in the submission system match the uploaded files.
5. Preserve the final submitted archive and submission-system confirmation.
