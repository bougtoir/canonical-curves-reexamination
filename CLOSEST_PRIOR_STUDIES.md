# Closest prior studies (JCPA citation ecology audit)

Focused audit of comparative-policy and adjacent scholarship most relevant
to this manuscript. Criterion: direct engagement with comparison,
lesson-drawing, policy transfer, or decision under uncertainty — no citation
added merely to cite JCPA.

| Study | What it contributes | How this manuscript differs | Cited? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rose (1991), Journal of Public Policy | Foundational lesson-drawing framework; transferability of programs across settings | Extends lesson-drawing from programs to appraisal of the evidence shapes decisions rest on | Yes |
