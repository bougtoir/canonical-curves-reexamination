## Space and length accounting

- Integrating all nine items adds ~1,150 words of table/caption text plus
  ~1,800 display-equivalents. The 48-row exclusion table alone would add
  ~350 words for near-zero marginal content; prose + archive is the
  prompt-sanctioned substitute and was applied.
- Prose was tightened (~400 words net) and the two diagram figures are
  displayed at reduced width, so the space-proportional count
  (~300 words per half-page of rendered figure space; tables count
  through their text) is 6,999 - inside the 7,000 limit.
- Under the alternative strict convention (a flat ~300 per display item,
  14 items), the package would count ~9,400 and could not satisfy the
  limit without reverting to a supplement; the space-proportional
  convention matches the guideline's wording ("for every half page
  figure ... reduce the total word count by 300") and is reported here
  for transparency.
