# JCPA fit audit

## Repositioning

- Before (Policy Studies): a decision-facing appraisal framework for
  evidence-based policymaking, motivated by a 50-relationship mapping and
  illustrated by two applications; comparison existed only as a synthesis
  subsection in the Discussion.
- After (JCPA): an explicitly comparative analysis. The comparative design
  is structurally central: a dedicated Comparative research design section,
  symmetric seven-subsection case structure, a Comparative findings section
  with propositions and lesson-drawing, and a comparative table (Table 5)
  whose final column extracts comparative lessons.

## JCPA fit elements

- Explicitly comparative: structured focused comparison; maximum-contrast
  case selection; common dimensions; comparative propositions.
- Public policy: emergency radiation protection and state fiscal policy.
- Lesson-drawing/extrapolation: dedicated subsection distinguishing
  transferable, context-dependent, and non-transferable lessons
  (Rose 1991; Dolowitz & Marsh 2000).
- Interdisciplinary: radiation epidemiology, public economics, comparative
  policy methods, model uncertainty.

## Changes by component

- Title: names comparison and both cases.
- Abstract: rewritten problem → comparison → comparative findings → lesson.
- Introduction: rebuilt around the comparative puzzle and contributions.
- Methods → Comparative research design: case selection, units, dimensions,
  evidence, inferential limits.
- Mapping: demoted to background motivation with calibrated interpretation.
- Case sections: reorganized into identical subheading sequences.
- Results → Comparative findings: common mechanism, divergences (Table 5),
  propositions, lesson-drawing.
- Discussion: comparative principal finding, what comparison adds,
  implications for comparative policy analysis, limitations.
- Cover letter: new JCPA letter; no prior-submission history; first-person.
