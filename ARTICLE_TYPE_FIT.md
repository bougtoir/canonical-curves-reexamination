# ARTICLE_TYPE_FIT — Evidence & Policy

## Candidate article types (journal guidance, retrieved 2026-10-30)

| Type | Limit | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Research article | 5,000-7,000 words incl. figures/tables | **Selected.** The manuscript is original primary research: an empirical cross-domain mapping plus two empirical applications (municipal vital statistics; state fiscal panels) analysed to study the evidence-to-policy relationship itself. |
| Methods article | 5,000-7,000 | Rejected: the contribution is not a new method; the appraisal checklist is a derived implication, not the primary research object. |
| Review article | 5,000-7,000 | Rejected: not a review of existing literature. |
| Debate article | 3,000-5,000 | Rejected: would force removal of the empirical analyses that constitute the primary research. |
| Practice article | ≤3,000 | Rejected: practice-facing lessons exist but are downstream of original research. |

## Decision: Research article

Rationale: the manuscript "describes an original piece of primary research that has relevance to the relationship between evidence and policy or practice", which is the journal's stated definition of a research article. The empirical contribution (a diagnostic applied to a purposive inventory of named relationships; two public-data applications) is exactly what distinguishes a research article from a debate or methods piece.

## Consequences for the package

- Structured abstract, ≤250 words, five labeled parts (Introduction; Methods/Data; Analytic plan; Results; Conclusion/Implications).
- Sections: Background; Methods; Findings; Discussion; Conclusion.
- ≤7,000 words including figures and tables at ~300 words per half-page display item. With ~5,000-5,200 words of text, the manuscript can carry ~6 half-page display items inline (4 figures + 2 tables). All remaining figures/tables (the mapping decision tree, event study, acute decomposition, neighbouring-state appendix figure, Fukushima ATT table, radiological-quantities table, Kansas synthetic-control table, appendix Tables A1/A2) move to an Online Supplement that does not count toward the limit.
- Custom Harvard references (author-date in text; italicized journal titles; no access dates).
- British English.
- Up to four key words.
- Double-anonymous manuscript.
