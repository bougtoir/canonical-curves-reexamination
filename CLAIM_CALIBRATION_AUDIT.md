## Calibrated wording

- Mapping: "purposive"; 34/50 bounded to mapped pairs, common diagnostic,
  observed range.
- Fukushima: measured municipal mortality, certified disaster-related
  deaths, and modeled radiological projections kept distinct and
  non-commensurate (Table 3 caption).
- Kansas: synthetic-control result described as a local treatment effect,
  not a general verdict on tax cuts.
- Comparative findings: differences attributed to the joint configuration
  of uncertainty, consequence asymmetry, reversibility, transportability,
  and learning capacity — not to statistical comparison of estimates.
