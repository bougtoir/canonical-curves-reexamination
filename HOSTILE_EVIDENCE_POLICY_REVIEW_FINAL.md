## Reviewer A — evidence-use scholar (E&P core readership)

**Objection.** "This is a statistics paper wearing an evidence-policy
costume. The 50-curve mapping and the quasi-experiments are
methodological; the evidence-use literature is window dressing."

**Class: INVALID-RESOLVED.** The framing is rebuilt around the
translation chain (evidence → assumed shape → decision → consequences);
the mapping is a diagnostic of an evidence-specificity gap; the
Discussion speaks separately to the journal's three audiences
(policymakers, researchers, knowledge brokers). Residual risk —
a referee could want more knowledge-broker literature — is disclosed;
the focused review cites the journal's own publications (Ward et al.
2009; Nutley et al. 2007).

## Reviewer B — knowledge-translation scholar

**Objection.** "A seven-question checklist is not a knowledge-
translation intervention; there is no evidence it changes appraisal
practice."

**Class: MINOR.** The checklist is offered explicitly as an
evidence-use tool, not a validated instrument — "an evidence-use tool,
not a universal theory". No effectiveness claim is made, so none must
be defended. The manuscript does not claim the checklist was
user-tested; that limitation is inherent and acknowledged by the
framing of implications, not hidden.

## Reviewer C — quantitative reviewer

**Objection.** "The quadratic nested-F diagnostic with Cook's-distance
and LOO-CV is ad hoc; 68% 'not robust' is an artifact of arbitrary
criteria."

**Class: INVALID-RESOLVED.** No prevalence claim is made ("a
diagnostic map, not a replication-failure rate"); the full 60–74%
sensitivity range is reported; the applications test identification
scope (only 6 observations below 0.1 Gy; turning point outside
observed rates), not estimate novelty. Hedges verified in text.

## Reviewer D — Fukushima / radiation-protection specialist

**Objection.** "Attacking LNT-based evacuation while ignoring the
precautionary rationale and tsunami confounding. Dangerous misreading
of health physics."

**Class: INVALID-RESOLVED.** Verbatim calibrations present: LNT not
declared false; evacuation not declared definitively wrong; measured,
certified, and projected deaths kept as distinct estimands (Table 4:
936 measured excess vs 2,350 certified related deaths vs THJ/WHO/
UNSCEAR projections, never commingled). Consequence asymmetry handled
in the synthesis; >10% inundation exclusion and T2 specification
address tsunami confounding.

## Reviewer E — public-finance economist

**Objection.** "Kansas is a straw man: nobody serious claimed Kansas
sat past the Laffer maximum; SCM placebo p=0.03 on IIT is cherry-picked
against GDP +1.5% (p=0.71)."

**Class: INVALID-RESOLVED.** All four outcomes with permutation
p-values are reported in the same paragraph — the asymmetry is shown,
not hidden. The claim is not "Kansas disproves Laffer" but that the
defence rested on local-position/slope propositions the evidence did
not identify, under assumptions about tax-rate–revenue and growth
responses (Laffer 2004; Rothschild 2012).
