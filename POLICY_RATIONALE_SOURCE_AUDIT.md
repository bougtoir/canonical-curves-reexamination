# Policy-rationale source audit (Evidence & Policy)

Every manuscript claim linking an evidence base to an assumed
functional shape and then to a decision is classified:

- **Documented** — a cited primary/secondary source makes the link.
- **Strongly supported** — the link follows from cited documents plus
  standard institutional knowledge, stated with hedged wording.
- **Interpretive** — the manuscript's own reconstruction; worded as
  reconstruction, not as documented fact.
- **Insufficiently documented** — would need removal or hedging.

## Fukushima application

| Claim | Sources | Class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICRP/UNSCEAR framework projects cancer risk at low doses under LNT-type assumptions | ICRP 2007; UNSCEAR 2013; WHO 2013 | Documented |
| Mandatory evacuations affected ~160,000 residents in T1 zone | Fukushima Prefecture 2014; Reconstruction Agency 2026 | Documented |
| Evacuation thresholds reflect dose-response assumptions applied to population risk projections | ICRP 2007; WHO 2013 | Strongly supported |
| Relocation carried its own mortality risks, esp. for vulnerable groups | Fukushima Prefecture certified related deaths (2,350, 2026-02-13); Tanigawa et al. 2012 | Documented |
| The decision depended on extrapolated low-dose slope more than on measured in-range effects | Manuscript reconstruction of the translation chain | Interpretive — worded as such ("the analysis isolates the functional-form proposition each decision depended on") |
| Evacuation is not declared wrong; LNT is not declared false | Calibration statements, Findings and Discussion | Documented (self-limitation) |

## Verdict

No **insufficiently documented** claims. All "policymakers relied
solely on a curve" framings were avoided; both applications state that
legal authority, politics, and multiple evidence streams drove the
decisions, and the analysis isolates only the functional-form
proposition. Interpretive links are marked as reconstruction in the
text, consistent with the calibrated-claim requirement.
